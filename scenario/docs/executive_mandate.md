# Executive Mandate — Project Catalyst

**From:** Anika Rao, Chief Technology Officer
**To:** AI Enablement Office
**Date:** Day 1
**Classification:** Internal / Confidential

---

## The decision

The Board has approved Project Catalyst: enterprise deployment of Claude Code to all ~10,000 Meridian software engineers within six months. Year-1 budget is **$14.0M**, held by this office.

## Why now

The Digital Banking pilot (200 engineers, 90 days) delivered results we can't ignore: 22% faster PR cycle time, 31% reduction in code-review rework, and a developer NPS of 71 — the highest of any tooling initiative we've run. Meanwhile JPMC, Goldman, and Barclays have all announced AI-assisted engineering programs. We are not going to be the bank that watched.

I'm aware of the pushback: the pilot team was greenfield, co-located, senior-skewing. Fair. That's exactly why this office exists — to prove it works across the messy reality of a global estate, or to find out fast that it doesn't.

## What I need from you

A six-month rollout plan I can defend to the Board, the CFO, and the Risk Committee in the same meeting. Specifically:

1. **Wave sequencing** grounded in data, not politics.
2. **A change and adoption model** that turns the 41% "cautious" into advocates and doesn't lose the 21% "concerned."
3. **Governance** that InfoSec and Compliance will sign. We are a bank. Act like it.
4. **An ROI framework** with a hard Month-3 checkpoint. David Okafor (CFO) was explicit: show measurable value at 90 days or the program pauses for review.
5. **The $14M allocated** with a rationale I can explain in one slide.
6. **Risks named**, not buried.

## Constraints

- No engineer is *required* to use Claude Code. Adoption must be earned.
- Existing InfoSec AI Tooling Policy (v2.3) applies until formally amended.
- Works Council consultation required before any rollout to Dublin or EU-based staff.
- No customer PII may transit any AI system without DPO sign-off.

## Success at Month 6

- ≥ 85% of the 10,000 have activated and used Claude Code in the prior 30 days
- ≥ 15% measured improvement in a velocity or quality metric per division
- Zero Sev-1 incidents attributable to AI-generated code
- A Year-2 plan the Board wants to fund

Make it happen.

— AR
