## Read-across for Meridian

- We are neither first nor last. The window to be a *credible* fast-follower is ~12 months.
- Every peer leads with **governance** in external comms — that's the license to operate in this sector.
- No peer has published a rigorous ROI methodology. If Catalyst produces one, it's a differentiator with the Board and the regulator.
- Apex's PR-heavy approach is a reputational risk *for them* if it doesn't hold up — and a competitive prod for our CEO.
