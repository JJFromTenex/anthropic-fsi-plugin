Three moves that make the math work:

1. **Two-speed licensing.** Not all 12,000 need full seats on Day 1. Model a ramped license curve (e.g., 3k → 6k → 9k → 12k active seats by quarter) instead of 12k flat. That alone recovers ~$1.5–2M against the original license line.

2. **Adjacent roles are your Month-3 ROI story, not your problem.** BAs and quants have near-zero governance overhead (no critical-function exposure), a 2-hour enablement path, and highly visible output (faster queries, faster models, faster specs). 3. **Cut white-glove training for the standard track.** The sentiment data says "proof on our codebase" and "seniors vouching" matter more than classroom hours. Shift to champion-led, in-repo enablement (cheap) and reserve instructor-led training for the critical-function cohort only (where attestation requires it anyway).

What you probably have to defer: full critical-function coverage by Month 6. Propose 60–70% of that cohort by M6 with a costed path to 100% by M9. The CRO will accept "slower and safe" over "fast and unsigned."
